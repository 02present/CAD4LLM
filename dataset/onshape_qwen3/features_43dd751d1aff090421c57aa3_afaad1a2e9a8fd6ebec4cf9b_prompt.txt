Create a main body by extruding a sketch along the XY plane 65 mm. Then remove material by extruding another sketch -62.5 mm, followed by removing more material with a -25 mm extrusion. Add a 25 mm deep cut on one side and a 2.5 mm deep cut on the other. Chamfer the edges with a 5 mm chamfer and round the corner with a 5 mm fillet. Finally, add two more extrusions of -7 mm and -65 mm to shape the front and top sections.
