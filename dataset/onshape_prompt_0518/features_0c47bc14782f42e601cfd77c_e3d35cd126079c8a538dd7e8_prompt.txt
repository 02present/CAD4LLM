Start by sketching a base shape on the XY plane. Extrude this sketch 2670 mm to create the main body. Apply a 100 mm fillet to the edges. Then, create additional sketches with varying line and arc entities on the XY plane and extrude them with different distances, some adding material and others removing it. Add fillets of 1.0 mm, 30 mm, and 50 mm as needed to refine the shape.
