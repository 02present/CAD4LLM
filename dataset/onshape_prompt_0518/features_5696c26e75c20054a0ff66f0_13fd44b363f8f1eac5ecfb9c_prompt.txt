Create a base plate by sketching a rectangular shape on the XY plane and extruding it 12 mm. Add a 5 mm fillet to the edges. Then, create track grooves by sketching smaller lines on the same plane and extruding them 25 mm to remove material. Drill riser holes by sketching multiple lines and extruding them 25 mm. Cut out the riser nut area with a 6 mm deep extrusion. Finally, add a top plate by sketching an arc and lines, extruding it 8 mm, and create support structures by extruding arcs 2 mm high.
