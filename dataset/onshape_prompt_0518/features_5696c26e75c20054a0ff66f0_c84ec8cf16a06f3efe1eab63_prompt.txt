Create a base shape by sketching a footprint with five lines on the XY plane and extruding it 10 mm. Then add an edge wall by sketching seven lines on the same plane and extruding it another 10 mm. Drill fixing holes through the base, then create a nut boss by extruding a small feature 2 mm. Remove material for a spiggot hole by extruding 25 mm, and add recesses for fixing nuts using a smaller sketch. Finally, apply fillets with radii of 3 mm, 2 mm, and 2.5 mm to smooth the edges.
