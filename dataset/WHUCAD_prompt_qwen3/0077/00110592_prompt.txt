Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 12mm, then extrude it 24mm to form a solid. Chamfer the edges of this extrusion. Next, sketch a circle centered at the bottom of the base and extrude it 120mm upwards to create a second body. Repeat the circular sketch on the same plane and extrude it another 120mm to form a third body.
