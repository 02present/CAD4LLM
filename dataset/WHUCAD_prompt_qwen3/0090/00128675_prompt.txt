Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular outline measuring 20mm wide and 95mm tall, then extrude it 96mm to create the main body. On the same XY plane, sketch a circle centered at the midpoint with a diameter of 96mm, extrude it 3mm to form a protrusion. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion process to add two more identical protrusions. Finally, chamfer the edges where the protrusions meet the main body.
