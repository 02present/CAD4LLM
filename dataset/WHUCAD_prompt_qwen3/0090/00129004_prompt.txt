Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane within a 95mm wide and 82mm tall area, then extrude it 56mm to form the main body. Chamfer the edges of the extruded face. Next, sketch a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 96mm, extrude it 40mm to create a secondary feature, and repeat the process with another circle to extrude 100mm for the final part.
