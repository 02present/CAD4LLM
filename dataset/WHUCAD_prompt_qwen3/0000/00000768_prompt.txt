Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions approximately 93mm by 77mm and extruding it 36mm. Then, chamfer two corners of the extruded face. Next, create a smaller sketch on the XY plane measuring around 73mm by 95mm and extrude it 9mm. Repeat this process with additional sketches of decreasing size, extruding each 2mm, to form a stepped structure.
