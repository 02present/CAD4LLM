Create a symmetrical part by first sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with four lines forming a 95mm by 59mm box. Revolve this sketch around the vertical axis to form a cylindrical shape. Then, create a circular sketch centered at the bottom of the cylinder and extrude it 14mm upwards to form a protrusion. Apply a chamfer to the edge where the protrusion meets the main body. Finally, repeat the circular sketch slightly shifted and extrude it another 14mm to create a second protrusion on the opposite side.
