Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 48mm and extruding it 7mm. Chamfer two corners of the base. Then sketch another set of six lines on the XY plane shifted down by 28mm and extrude it 7mm. Sketch a circle in the middle of the lower section and extrude it 125mm upwards, then sketch another circle slightly larger and extrude it 125mm as well.
