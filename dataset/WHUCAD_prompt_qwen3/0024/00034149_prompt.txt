Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 50mm and extruding it 17mm. Then, chamfer two corners of this base. Next, sketch another rectangle on the XY plane measuring 63mm by 95mm and extrude it 111mm. Repeat this process with a new rectangle of 95mm by 61mm and a circular sketch with a diameter of 96mm, each extruded 111mm.
