Create a base shape by sketching an arc and lines on the XY plane with a bounding box of 0 to 95 mm in X and -16 to 60 mm in Y, then extrude it 96 mm to form a body. Repeat this process for three additional sketches on the same plane, each with different dimensions and shapes, and extrude each to create separate bodies. Finally, chamfer the edges where they meet.
