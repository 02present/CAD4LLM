Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 80 mm and extruding it 20 mm. Then, chamfer each corner of the resulting body. Repeat this process for two more circles, slightly smaller and positioned inward, and chamfer each of those as well. Finally, add a complex sketch with arcs and lines on the same plane and extrude it 20 mm, then chamfer its edges.
