Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 63mm and extruding it 4mm. Then, create a second rectangular sketch on the XY plane measuring 95mm by 6mm and extrude it 28mm. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Add two more identical rectangular extrusions of 28mm each, followed by two circular sketches with diameters of 12mm and 2mm respectively, each extruded 12mm.
