Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane and extruding them 40 mm to form two separate bodies. Repeat the process with another set of four lines and extrude again 40 mm. Chamfer the edges of both extrusions. Then sketch a more complex profile with lines and arcs, extrude it 40 mm, and chamfer its edges as well. Finally, create two circular sketches and extrude each 29 mm to form cylindrical features.
