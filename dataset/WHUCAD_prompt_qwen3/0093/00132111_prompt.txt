Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm wide and 13mm high rectangle on the XY plane and extruding it 2mm. Then, chamfer the edges. Add three more extrusions: first a 2mm wide and 95mm high feature extruded 1mm, followed by a 29mm wide and 95mm high feature also extruded 1mm, and finally a 95mm wide and 76mm high feature extruded 10mm. All sketches should be on the XY plane.
