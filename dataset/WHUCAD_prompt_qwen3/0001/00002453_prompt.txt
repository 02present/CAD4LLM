Create a base shape by sketching a square on the XY plane with 5 lines forming a 95x95mm square and extruding it 96mm. Then add four chamfers to the edges. On the same plane, sketch a circle centered at the origin with a diameter of 96mm and extrude it 69mm. Repeat this process with smaller circles and extrusions of 75mm, 69mm, 66mm, and 32mm, adding two more chamfers to the final features.
