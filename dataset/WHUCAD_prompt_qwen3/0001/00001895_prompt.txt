Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 0 to 96 in the X direction and -48 to 48 in the Y direction, then extrude it 34 millimeters to form a new body. Chamfer the edge of this extrusion. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 48 millimeters to create a second separate body.
