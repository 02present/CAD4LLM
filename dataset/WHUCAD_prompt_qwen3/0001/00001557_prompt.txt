Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 54mm and extruding it 8mm. Then, create a smaller rectangle of 95mm by 48mm and extrude it 5mm, followed by a 95mm by 40mm rectangle extruded 2mm, and finally a 95mm by 50mm rectangle extruded 7mm. Chamfer the edges of the first two extrusions.
