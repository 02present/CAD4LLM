Create a base shape by sketching an 8-line and arc profile on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 50mm, then extrude it 14mm. Add a chamfer to the edge. Next, sketch a 5-entity line and arc pattern on the same plane, larger in height, and extrude it 14mm. Repeat this process with smaller sketches, extruding them 114mm and 8mm respectively to form the final assembly.
