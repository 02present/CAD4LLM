Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 97 mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. On the same plane, sketch four lines to form a small rectangular profile and revolve it around an axis. Sketch another circle, extrude it 97 mm, then sketch a circle again and extrude it 51 mm. Finally, sketch four lines for a top profile and extrude it 37 mm.
