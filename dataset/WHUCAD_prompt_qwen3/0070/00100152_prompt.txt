Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane with dimensions 18mm by 95mm and extruding it 5mm. Then, create a second shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 26mm and extruding it 5mm. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Repeat the process to create two more identical shapes and extrude them 5mm each.
