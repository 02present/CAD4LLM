Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 0 to 96 in X and -48 to 48 in Y, then extrude it 32 mm to form the first body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Repeat by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 58 mm to create a second body, and finally sketch one more circle and extrude it 6 mm to make the third body.
