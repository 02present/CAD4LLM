Create a base shape by sketching eight arcs on the XY plane with a bounding box from 0 to 90 mm in X and -6 to 80 mm in Y, then extrude it 6 mm to form the main body. Chamfer two edges of the base. On the same XY plane, sketch five circles spaced evenly within a 96 mm diameter circle and extrude each 9 mm to create five identical protrusions.
