Create a base body by extruding a circle with a 10 mm diameter on the XY plane 10 mm upwards. Then, add a second extrusion of a larger circle with a 5 mm diameter also on the XY plane but only 5 mm high. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Continue by extruding three more complex sketches, each containing lines and arcs, to form additional features, all 5 mm tall. Finally, create a symmetrical circular protrusion using another extrusion on the same plane.
