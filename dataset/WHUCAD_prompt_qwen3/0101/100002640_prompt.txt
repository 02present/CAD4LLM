Create a base extrusion by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 32mm and extruding it 8mm. Then, create seven additional sketches on the XY plane at different positions along the 95mm length, each forming a smaller rectangle of varying widths (6mm, 28mm, 50mm, 73mm, 95mm, and two more) and a fixed height of 17mm.
