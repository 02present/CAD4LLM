Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm side length and extruding it 50mm. Chamfer the edges of the base. Then, create two small circular sketches on the XY plane, extrude them 2mm and 25mm respectively. Finally, add a detailed sketch with lines and an arc on the XY plane and extrude it 19mm to form the final feature.
