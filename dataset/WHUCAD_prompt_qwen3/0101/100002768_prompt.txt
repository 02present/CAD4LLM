Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with dimensions 95mm by 32mm. Extrude this sketch 48mm to create a solid body. Repeat the process with a smaller rectangle of 84mm by 24mm and extrude it 48mm as a separate body. Add two more shallow extrusions of 6mm each from the same initial sketch and place circular sketches at the ends for detailing.
