Create three extruded cylinders by sketching circles on the XY plane and extruding them outward. The first circle has a diameter of about 58 mm and is extruded 17 mm, the second has a diameter of about 88 mm and is extruded 11 mm, and the third has a diameter of about 96 mm and is also extruded 11 mm. All sketches should be placed on the XY plane with appropriate positioning.
