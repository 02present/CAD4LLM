Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm side length and extruding it 24mm. Then chamfer three edges of the resulting body. On the same XY plane, sketch a circle centered at the origin with a diameter slightly smaller than the square and extrude it 8mm to create a smaller cylindrical feature. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion to add another identical cylindrical feature.
