Create three extruded cylinders by sketching circles on the XY plane and extruding them outward. The first circle has a diameter of about 58 mm and is extruded 36 mm, the second circle is slightly smaller with a diameter of around 56 mm and is extruded 92 mm, and the third circle is larger with a diameter of approximately 96 mm and is also extruded 92 mm. All extrusions should create new bodies.
