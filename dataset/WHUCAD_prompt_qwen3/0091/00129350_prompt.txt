Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 68mm and extruding it 35mm. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch three lines on the XY plane shifted down by 38mm and extrude this sketch 81mm. Chamfer the resulting edges again. Sketch a circle in the middle of the base and extrude it 17mm, then repeat with another circle positioned lower and extrude it 17mm as well. Chamfer each of these last two extrusions.
