Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a bounding box of 63mm by 95mm, then extrude it 4mm to form a solid. Chamfer the edges of the base. On the same plane, sketch another set of four lines forming a smaller rectangle of 2mm by 95mm and extrude it 127mm to create a taller section. Repeat the sketch and extrusion for a third identical smaller section.
