Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 14 mm and extruding it 17 mm to form a cylinder. Repeat this process for another circle positioned 17 mm to the right, creating a second cylinder of the same size. Chamfer both ends of each cylinder. Then, create a rectangular sketch with four lines on the XY plane and extrude it 21 mm to form a larger block. Finally, create a smaller rectangular sketch and extrude it 5 mm to add a final detail.
