Create a symmetrical part by first sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 6 to 90 mm in the X direction and -42 to 42 mm in the Y direction. Extrude this sketch 96 mm to form a new body. Repeat the sketch and extrusion process on the opposite side to create a mirrored feature. Finally, chamfer the edges where the two extruded bodies meet.
