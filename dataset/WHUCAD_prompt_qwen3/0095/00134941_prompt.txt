Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm wide and 3mm high rectangle on the XY plane and extruding it 1mm. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch another 48mm wide and 95mm high rectangle on the XY plane and extrude it 1mm. Finally, sketch a 95mm wide and 3mm high rectangle again and extrude it 19mm to form the final shape.
