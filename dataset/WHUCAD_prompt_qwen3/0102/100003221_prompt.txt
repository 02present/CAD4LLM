Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with dimensions 95mm by 27mm. Extrude this sketch 17mm to create the first body. Then, sketch another rectangle of 95mm by 3mm and extrude it 73mm to form the second body. Finally, sketch two sets of circles on the XY plane and extrude the last sketch 91mm to create the third body, and chamfer the edges as needed.
