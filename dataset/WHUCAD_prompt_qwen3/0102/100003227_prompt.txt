Create a base shape by sketching four lines and an arc on the XY plane, then revolve it around an axis to form a symmetrical body. Chamfer two edges of the resulting shape. On the same plane, sketch a circle and extrude it 48 mm to create a new body. Repeat by sketching additional circles and extruding them 30 mm and 48 mm respectively, finally chamfering another edge to complete the design.
