Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a bounding box of 95mm by 48mm, then extrude it 19mm to form the first body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Next, sketch an arc and a line on the XY plane, extrude that sketch 32mm to create a second body, and repeat the process with another arc and line sketch to make a third extruded body.
