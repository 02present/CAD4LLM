Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular profile and extruding it 21 mm. Then, add a circular sketch on the XY plane and extrude it 21 mm as a separate body. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Repeat the rectangular sketch and extrude it 64 mm, followed by two circular sketches extruded 86 mm each to form the final structure.
