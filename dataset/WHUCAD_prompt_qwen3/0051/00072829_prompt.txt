Create a base shape by sketching three lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular outline and extruding it 18 mm. Then, create a circular sketch on the XY plane and extrude it 18 mm to form a separate feature. Chamfer the edge where the two features meet. Add a larger circular sketch and extrude it 100 mm, followed by two more linear sketches extruded 100 mm each to complete the structure.
