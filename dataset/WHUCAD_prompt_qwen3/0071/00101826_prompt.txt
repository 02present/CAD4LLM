Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 30 to 50 mm in width and 36 to 56 mm in height, then extrude it 14 mm to form a solid body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Next, sketch four lines and arcs on the XY plane with a larger bounding box, extrude each sketch 100 mm to create two additional solid bodies.
