Create a base shape by sketching an arc and line pattern on the XY plane, then extrude it 4 mm to form a small protrusion. Chamfer the edges of this feature. Next, sketch a circle on the XY plane and extrude it 124 mm to create a tall cylinder. Repeat the sketch and extrude process three more times with smaller extrusions of 1 mm each, positioning them around the central cylinder.
