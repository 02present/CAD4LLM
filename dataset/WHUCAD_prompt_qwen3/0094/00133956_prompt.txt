Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 36 mm and extruding it 3 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch another smaller circle on the same plane, centered and slightly larger than the first, and extrude it 3 mm to create a second cylinder. Finally, sketch a more complex shape consisting of arcs and lines on the XY plane and extrude it 3 mm to form the third part of the assembly.
