Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 32 mm and extruding it 19 mm to form a cylinder. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular profile and extrude it 109 mm to create a larger block. Finally, repeat the circular sketch and extrude it another 109 mm to form a second cylinder aligned with the first.
