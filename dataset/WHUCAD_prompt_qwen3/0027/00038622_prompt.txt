Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm bounding box and extruding it 96mm. Then, chamfer the edges. On the same plane, sketch a circle centered at the origin with a diameter of 96mm and extrude it 32mm. Repeat with a smaller circle and extrude it 24mm. Finally, add another set of four lines and extrude them 24mm to complete the model.
