Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 6 to 90 mm in both directions and extruding it 15 mm. Then, chamfer the edge. Next, sketch another circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 7 to 89 mm horizontally and -41 to 41 mm vertically, and extrude this second sketch 15 mm to form a second body.
