Create a base shape by sketching an 84mm by 56mm rectangle with arcs and lines on the XY plane, then extrude it 19mm. Add a chamfer to the edge. Sketch another symmetrical arc and line pattern on the XY plane and extrude it 19mm. Repeat this process for two more symmetrical sketches, extruding each 19mm, and finally create a wider sketch on the XY plane and extrude it 23mm to form the final part.
