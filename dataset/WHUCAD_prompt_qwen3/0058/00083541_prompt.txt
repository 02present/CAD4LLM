Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with six lines, then extrude it 3 mm to form a solid. Repeat by sketching another rectangle on the same plane with five lines and extruding it 3 mm to create a second solid. Chamfer the edge where the two solids meet. Finally, sketch a smaller shape with lines and arcs on the XY plane and extrude it 1 mm to add a detail feature.
