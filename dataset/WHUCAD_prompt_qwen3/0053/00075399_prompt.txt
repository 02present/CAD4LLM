Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 61mm and extruding it 3mm. Then, create a vertical rib by sketching a smaller rectangle 6mm wide and 95mm tall, extruding it 66mm. Add a thin horizontal rib by sketching a 95mm long and 3mm tall rectangle and extruding it 66mm. Repeat the vertical rib pattern on both sides and add a chamfer to one of the edges.
