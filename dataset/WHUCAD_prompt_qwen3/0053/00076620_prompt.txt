Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 0 to 96 in X and -48 to 48 in Y, then extrude it 9 mm to form the first body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane, extruding it 15 mm for the second body, and finally sketching a third circle and extruding it 122 mm to create the final body.
