Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 30 mm to form a solid. Then, chamfer the edges of the resulting body. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 30 mm to create a second solid. Finally, sketch a more complex shape with lines and arcs on the XY plane and extrude it 30 mm to form the third and final part of the model.
