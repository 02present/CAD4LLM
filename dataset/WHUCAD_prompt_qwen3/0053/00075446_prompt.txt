Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 30 mm and extruding it 67 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular shape about 95 mm wide and 44 mm tall, and extrude this sketch 67 mm to create a second block. Finally, chamfer the corners of the second block.
