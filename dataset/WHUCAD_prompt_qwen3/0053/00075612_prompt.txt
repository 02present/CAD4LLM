Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 88 mm and extruding it 7 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular pattern measuring 6 mm by 95 mm and extrude this sketch 45 mm to create a larger block. Repeat the process with another set of four lines arranged vertically to form a 95 mm by 6 mm rectangle and extrude it 45 mm as well. Finally, chamfer the outer edges of the final assembly.
