Create a base shape by extruding a sketch with four lines on the XY plane, 95mm wide and 2mm tall, 1mm deep. Then add another extrusion of a slightly larger sketch, 95mm wide and 1mm tall, also 1mm deep. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Finally, create two more extrusions from additional sketches, one 95mm wide and 95mm tall, and another 1mm wide and 95mm tall, each 1mm deep.
