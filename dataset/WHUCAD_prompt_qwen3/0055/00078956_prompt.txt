Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 16 mm and extruding it 19 mm. Repeat this process for another circle slightly offset, also extruding 19 mm. Add two chamfers to the edges of the main body. Then create a more complex sketch with lines and an arc, extrude it 19 mm to form a larger structure, and finally add a small protrusion by sketching a circle and extruding it just 1 mm.
