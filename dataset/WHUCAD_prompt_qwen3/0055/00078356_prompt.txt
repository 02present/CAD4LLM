Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane within a 95mm wide and 82mm tall area, then extrude it 64mm to form a solid body. Chamfer three corners of the extruded face with equal depth. On the same plane, sketch a circle with a diameter slightly larger than the original sketch and extrude it 32mm to create a second protrusion. Finally, chamfer the last corner of the second extrusion.
