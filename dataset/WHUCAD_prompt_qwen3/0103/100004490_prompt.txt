Create a base body by extruding a 4mm deep sketch consisting of four lines on the XY plane from 0 to 57mm in the X direction and 0 to 95mm in the Y direction. Then, extrude a 4mm deep circular sketch at the bottom center and another at the top center, as well as two more circles on the sides, to form a symmetrical structure. Finally, extrude a 25mm deep sketch with five lines across the full width to create the main body, and chamfer the edges for a finished look.
