Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with width 30 mm and height 91 mm, then extrude it 12 mm to form a solid block. Chamfer the edges of this block. Next, sketch two lines on the XY plane to create a longer horizontal line and extrude it 68 mm to form a second body. Finally, sketch an arc and a line on the same plane and extrude that to create a third body.
