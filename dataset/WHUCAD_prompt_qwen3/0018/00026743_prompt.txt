Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 8mm and extruding it 4mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular outline measuring approximately 95mm by 33mm and extrude this sketch 16mm to create a larger base. Finally, sketch another set of four lines for a smaller rectangle, around 95mm by 20mm, and extrude it 126mm to form the final tall section of the model.
