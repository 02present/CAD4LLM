Create a base shape by sketching five lines on the XY plane and extruding them 30 mm to form a body. Then, chamfer four corners of the resulting face. On the same plane, sketch another set of four lines forming a smaller rectangle, extrude it 30 mm to create a second body, and chamfer one corner. Finally, sketch a larger rectangle and extrude it 17 mm to make a third body, then chamfer its last corner.
