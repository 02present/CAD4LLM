Create a base body by extruding a circle with a diameter of about 14 mm along the Z-axis for 15 mm. Then, on the same plane, sketch three lines forming a triangular shape and extrude them 15 mm to create a second body. Repeat this process two more times using different line arrangements, each time extruding 15 mm. Finally, add a larger circle in the center and extrude it 15 mm to form the final feature.
