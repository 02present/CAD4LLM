Create a base body by extruding a sketch on the XY plane with seven lines and an arc, 100 mm tall. Add another extrusion of a smaller sketch on the same plane, 100 mm tall, and repeat this pattern for three extrusions. Chamfer the edges of the first three extruded bodies. Then create two more extrusions using different sketches, each 28 mm tall, and chamfer their edges as well.
