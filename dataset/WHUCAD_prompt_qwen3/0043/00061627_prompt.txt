Create a base shape by extruding a sketch of four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 18mm. Then, extrude a circular sketch below it to form a second feature. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Repeat the initial sketch and extrusion pattern vertically to create multiple layers, with some extrusions extending much longer than others. Add additional circular features and extrude them to complete the assembly.
