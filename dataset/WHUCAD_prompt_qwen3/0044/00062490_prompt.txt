Create a base shape by sketching four lines and an arc on the XY plane, then extrude it 3 mm. Repeat this process with a new sketch containing a line, arc, line, and line, extruding it another 3 mm. Add a third extrusion using a sketch with two arcs and two lines, also 3 mm thick. Finally, create a circular sketch in the center and extrude it 7 mm to form a hole.
