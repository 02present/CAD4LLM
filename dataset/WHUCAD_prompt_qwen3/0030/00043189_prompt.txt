Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 34 mm, then extrude it 11 mm to form a cylinder. Chamfer the edges of the cylinder. On the same XY plane, sketch an intricate pattern of arcs and lines, and revolve it around the central axis to create a symmetrical feature. Finally, chamfer the outer edges of the revolved shape for a polished look.
