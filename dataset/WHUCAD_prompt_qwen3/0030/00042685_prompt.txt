Create a symmetrical part by first sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with four lines forming a 95mm by 38mm box. Revolve this sketch around the vertical axis to form a cylindrical shape. Chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Then, create a larger square sketch on the same plane and extrude it 14mm to form a separate body. Finally, draw a circle in the center of the XY plane and extrude it 14mm to create a rounded protrusion.
