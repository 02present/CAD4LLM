Create two extruded cylinders by sketching circles on the XY plane and extruding them 48 mm. The first circle should have a bounding box of 30 to 66 mm in the X direction and -18 to 18 mm in the Y direction, while the second circle should have a bounding box of 16 to 80 mm in the X direction and -32 to 32 mm in the Y direction. Both extrusions should create new bodies and be blind.
