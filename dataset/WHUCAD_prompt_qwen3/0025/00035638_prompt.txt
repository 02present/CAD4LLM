Create a base shape by sketching an asymmetrical pattern of arcs and lines on the XY plane, then extrude it 96 mm to form the main body. Add a circular sketch on the same plane and extrude it 96 mm to create a secondary feature. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion process three more times, adjusting the extrusion depth to 64 mm for the last one.
