Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane with a width of 15mm and height of 95mm, then extrude it 8mm to form a solid body. Chamfer the edges of the extruded face. On the same plane, sketch another set of six lines forming a 33mm wide by 95mm tall shape and revolve it around an axis to create a symmetrical feature. Repeat the extrusion and revolution process for additional symmetrically placed features.
