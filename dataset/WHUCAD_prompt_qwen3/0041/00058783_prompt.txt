Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 60 mm and extruding it 28 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch four connected lines and arcs on the XY plane forming a symmetrical pattern, and extrude this sketch 28 mm to create a second body. Finally, chamfer the edges of the second extruded feature as well.
