Create a base extrusion by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 17mm and extruding it 96mm. Then, chamfer two corners of the extrusion. Repeat the process by sketching another rectangle rotated 90 degrees, extruding it 96mm, and chamfering one corner. Finally, create a smaller extrusion using a slightly smaller rectangle and extrude it 10mm.
