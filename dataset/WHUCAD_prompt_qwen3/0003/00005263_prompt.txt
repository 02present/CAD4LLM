Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from [0.0, -48.0, 96.0, 48.0], then extrude it 10 mm to form a cylinder. Chamfer the edges of this cylinder. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 38 mm to create a second cylinder, and do the same again for a third cylinder.
