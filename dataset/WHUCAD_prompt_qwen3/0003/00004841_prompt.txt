Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 29mm, then extrude it 15mm to form a solid body. Chamfer two corners of this body. Repeat the process by sketching four lines on the XY plane with dimensions 33mm by 95mm, extruding it 15mm to create another solid body, and chamfer its corners as well. Continue this pattern to build five extruded features with similar dimensions but varying sketch orientations and positions.
