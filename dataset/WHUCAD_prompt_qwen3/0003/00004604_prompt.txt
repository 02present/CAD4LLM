Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a bounding box of 95mm by 27mm, then extrude it 2mm to form a new body. Chamfer the edges of this extrusion. Next, sketch another set of four lines on the XY plane, shifted down by 33mm with a larger bounding box of 95mm by 66mm, and extrude it 2mm to create a second body. Repeat this process one more time with a smaller sketch on the same plane and extrude it 2mm as well.
