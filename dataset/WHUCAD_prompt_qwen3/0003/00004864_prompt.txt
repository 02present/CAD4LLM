Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 61mm and extruding it 2mm. Then, create a second rectangular sketch on the XY plane measuring 3mm by 95mm and extrude it 94mm. Chamfer two corners of the main body. Repeat the process for additional sketches on the XY plane with dimensions 3mm by 95mm and 95mm by 3mm, extruding them 94mm and 96mm respectively.
