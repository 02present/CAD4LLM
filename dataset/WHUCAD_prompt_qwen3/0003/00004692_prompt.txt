Create a base shape by sketching a complex profile on the XY plane with 12 lines and arcs, then extrude it 68 mm to form the main body. On the same plane, sketch a circle and extrude it 28 mm to create a protrusion. Chamfer the edge where the protrusion meets the main body. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion process five times around the main body to form symmetric features.
