Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 21mm, then extrude it 6mm to form a solid body. On the same plane, sketch another set of four lines forming a 15mm by 95mm rectangle, extrude it 13mm to create a second body, and repeat the sketch and extrusion one more time for a third body. Finally, chamfer each of the three extruded bodies at the corners.
