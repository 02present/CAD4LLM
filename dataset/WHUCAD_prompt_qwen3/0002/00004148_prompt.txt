Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with dimensions 95mm by 47mm, then extrude it 8mm to form a solid. Chamfer two of the edges. On the same plane, sketch a circle and extrude it 4mm to create a protrusion. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion to add another similar protrusion. Finally, sketch another rectangle rotated 90 degrees and extrude it 111mm to complete the structure.
