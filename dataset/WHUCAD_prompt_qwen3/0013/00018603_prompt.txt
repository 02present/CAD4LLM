Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 50 mm and extruding it 17 mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Next, sketch a more complex profile with arcs and lines on the XY plane and extrude it 5 mm. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion process twice more at 11 mm each, adding another chamfer between the second and third extrusions.
