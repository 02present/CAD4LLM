Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 28mm and height of 95mm, then extrude it 9mm to form the main body. Repeat the process with smaller sketches of 4mm width to create additional protrusions, each extruded 9mm, and finally create two longer protrusions by extruding 98mm from the last two small sketches. Chamfer the edges where they meet.
