Create a base shape by extruding a sketch on the XY plane with four lines spanning 21mm wide and 95mm tall, then extrude it 45mm. Add another extrusion of a six-line sketch on the same plane, 95mm wide and 14mm tall, also 45mm deep. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Repeat this process with additional sketches and extrusions, varying widths and heights, and extruding some 90mm instead of 45mm.
