Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 16 to 80 in the X direction and -32 to 32 in the Y direction, then extrude it 21 millimeters to form the main body. Repeat the process by sketching another circle in the same location and extruding it 3 millimeters to create a smaller protrusion, and do the same again for a third extrusion of 3 millimeters.
