Create a base by sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 19 mm. Then, add two chamfers. On the same plane, sketch three connected lines and an arc, extrude this shape 96 mm to form a larger body. Repeat the sketch and extrusion for another identical shape, then apply a second chamfer. Finally, sketch a more complex shape with arcs and lines and extrude it 9 mm to complete the model.
