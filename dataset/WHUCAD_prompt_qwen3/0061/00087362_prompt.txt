Create a base body by extruding a circle with a diameter of 80 mm along the Z-axis for 11 mm. Then, create a second identical extrusion on top of the first one, offset by 14 mm in the X-direction, also 11 mm tall. Add a chamfer to the corner where the two bodies meet, and finally, extrude a larger circle with a diameter of 80 mm vertically for 114 mm to form the main structure. Apply another chamfer to the top edge of the tallest extrusion.
