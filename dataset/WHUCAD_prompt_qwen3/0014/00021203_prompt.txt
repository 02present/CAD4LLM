Create a base shape by sketching eight lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with dimensions 59mm by 95mm and extruding it 54mm. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch four lines on the same plane to form a smaller rectangle and extrude it 26mm, followed by another chamfer. Sketch a circle in the center of the base and extrude it 116mm, then repeat the circle sketch and extrude it 123mm to create a stacked structure.
