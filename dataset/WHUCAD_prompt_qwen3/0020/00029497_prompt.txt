Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 30 mm and extruding it 70 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch four lines and an arc on the XY plane to form a profile, extrude it 11 mm to create a second feature, and chamfer its edges as well. Repeat the sketch and extrusion process for a third feature using the same profile and extrusion depth.
