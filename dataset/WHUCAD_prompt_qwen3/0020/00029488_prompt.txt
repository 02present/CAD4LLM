Create a base shape by sketching a series of lines on the XY plane with dimensions approximately 95mm by 86mm, then extrude it 16mm to form the first body. Next, chamfer the edges. On the same plane, sketch a smaller rectangle about 63mm by 95mm and extrude it 32mm to create a second body. Finally, sketch another set of lines on the XY plane measuring roughly 95mm by 71mm and extrude it 48mm to complete the third body.
