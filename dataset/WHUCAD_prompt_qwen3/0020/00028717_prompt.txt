Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane within a 190mm wide and 145mm tall area, then extrude it 7mm to form a solid body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Next, sketch a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 96mm, extrude it 121mm to create a second body, and finally sketch another circle of the same size and extrude it 114mm to form a third body.
