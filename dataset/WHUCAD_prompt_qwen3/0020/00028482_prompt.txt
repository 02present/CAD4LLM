Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 8mm and extruding it 5mm to form a cylinder. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Next, sketch a larger circle on the XY plane with a diameter of around 96mm and extrude it 10mm to create a second cylinder. Repeat the sketch and extrusion process one more time to produce a third cylinder of the same size.
