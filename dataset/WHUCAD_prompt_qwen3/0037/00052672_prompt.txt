Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle with dimensions 48mm by 95mm, then extrude it 48mm to form a solid. Chamfer the edges of the base. Next, create a circular sketch on the XY plane with a diameter slightly smaller than the base, and extrude it 32mm to form a second body. Repeat the circular sketch and extrusion to create a third identical body.
