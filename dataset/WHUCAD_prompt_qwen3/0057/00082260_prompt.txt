Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 44 mm. Then, add two chamfers to the edges. Next, sketch four connected lines and arcs on the XY plane and extrude them 44 mm. Repeat this process with another circle and extrusion, followed by another chamfer. Finally, sketch eight lines on the XY plane and extrude them 22 mm to complete the design.
