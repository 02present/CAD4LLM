Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 59 mm and extruding it 16 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer two edges of the cylinder. On the same plane, sketch another smaller circle with a diameter of about 36 mm and extrude it 16 mm to create a second cylinder. Finally, sketch two sets of lines on the XY plane and extrude each set 32 mm to form two additional rectangular blocks.
