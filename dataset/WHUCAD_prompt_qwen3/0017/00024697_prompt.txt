Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 7 mm and extruding it 3 mm. Then, add two chamfers to the edges. Repeat the process by sketching another circle of the same size and extruding it 5 mm, followed by a second chamfer. Finally, sketch a larger circle spanning 96 mm in width and extrude it 4 mm to form the final part.
