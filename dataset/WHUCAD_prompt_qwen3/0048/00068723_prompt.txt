Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 10mm and extruding it 7mm. Then, create a larger circular sketch on the same plane with a diameter of around 96mm and extrude it 53mm. Repeat this process for another circle, also extruding it 53mm. Add chamfers to the edges of the largest features. Finally, add smaller rectangular sketches on the XY plane and extrude them 14mm and 8mm respectively, then chamfer the outer edges of these smaller features.
