Create a symmetrical part by first sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 98 mm to form a cylinder. Chamfer two corners of the cylinder. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 98 mm, then chamfer the third corner. Finally, sketch a more complex shape with lines and arcs on the XY plane and extrude it 113 mm to complete the design.
