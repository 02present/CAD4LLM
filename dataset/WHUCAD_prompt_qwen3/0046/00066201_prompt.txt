Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 40 mm and extruding it 3 mm to form a protrusion. Then, chamfer the edge of this protrusion. On the same plane, sketch another slightly larger circle and extrude it 1 mm to create a smaller protrusion, repeating this process one more time with a marginally larger circle and another 1 mm extrusion.
