Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane within a 95mm wide and 82mm tall area, then extrude it 38mm to form the main body. Chamfer the edges of the main body. Next, sketch a circle on the XY plane with a diameter slightly larger than the main body, extrude it 2mm to create a small protrusion. Repeat the process with another circle and extrude it 56mm to form a taller feature.
