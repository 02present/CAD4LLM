Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 16 mm and extruding it 11 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer two edges of the cylinder. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular pattern and extrude them 107 mm to create a larger base. Finally, sketch another set of four lines on the XY plane and extrude them 111 mm to form the final main body of the part.
