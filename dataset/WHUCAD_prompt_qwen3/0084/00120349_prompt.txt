Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm long and 6mm high profile on the XY plane, then extrude it 1mm. Add chamfers to three corners of the resulting body. Repeat the process with a taller sketch of 28mm height, extruding again 1mm, and apply additional chamfers to the new edges. Finally, create a third extrusion using a similar 6mm tall sketch and chamfer its edges as well.
