Start by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with six lines forming a slightly wider base and narrower top. Extrude this sketch 64 mm to create the main body. Then chamfer the edges. Next, sketch a smaller rectangle on the same plane and extrude it 45 mm. Repeat the chamfer, followed by another sketch and extrusion of 90 mm to form the final feature. Finally, revolve the last sketch around an axis to complete the shape.
