Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm wide and 12mm tall profile on the XY plane, then extrude it 23mm. Add chamfers to the edges. Repeat the process by sketching smaller profiles along the vertical direction and extruding each 23mm to form a series of stacked features. Finally, create a top extrusion with a 95mm width and 10mm height, also extruded 23mm.
