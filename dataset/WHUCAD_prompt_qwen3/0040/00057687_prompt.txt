Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 10mm, then extrude it 2mm to form a solid. Chamfer the edges of this extrusion. On the same XY plane, sketch another set of four lines forming a 16mm wide by 95mm tall rectangle, extrude it 7mm to create a second solid, and repeat the sketch and extrusion process once more to form a third solid.
