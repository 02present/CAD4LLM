Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from [0.0, -48.0, 96.0, 48.0], then extrude it 16 mm to form the first body. Chamfer the edges of this body. Repeat by sketching another circle on the same plane and extruding it 28 mm to create a second body, and do the same again for a third extrusion.
