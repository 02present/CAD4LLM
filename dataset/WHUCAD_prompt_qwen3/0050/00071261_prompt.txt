Create a base shape by sketching four lines and arcs on the XY plane and extruding it 12 mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. On another section of the XY plane, sketch four lines and extrude that 12 mm as well. Further up, sketch an arc and two lines, extrude them 104 mm, and repeat with another arc and two lines, also extruding 104 mm.
