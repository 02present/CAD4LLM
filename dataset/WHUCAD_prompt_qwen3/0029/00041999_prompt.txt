Create a base shape by sketching an arc and four lines on the XY plane, then extrude it 5 mm to form a solid. On the same plane, sketch a circle, extrude it 5 mm to create a second feature, and chamfer its edges. Repeat by sketching another circle, extruding it 16 mm, chamfering again, and finally extruding a fourth circle 16 mm to complete the design.
