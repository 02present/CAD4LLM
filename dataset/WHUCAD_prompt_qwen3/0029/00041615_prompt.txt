Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm wide and 24mm tall profile on the XY plane and extruding it 96mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Next, create a taller sketch on the same plane measuring 95mm wide and 63mm tall, and extrude it 64mm. Repeat this process with shorter sketches of 95mm width and 71mm height, extruding each 32mm.
