Create a base shape by sketching a series of lines on the XY plane and extruding them 17 mm to form a solid. Add a circular sketch on the same plane and extrude it 17 mm to create a second feature. Chamfer the edges where they meet. Then, create additional features by sketching lines and arcs, extruding them 17 mm, and finally adding two larger circular extrusions of 120 mm each.
