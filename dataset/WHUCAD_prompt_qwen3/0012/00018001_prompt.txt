Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 50 mm and extruding it 17 mm. Then, add a second extrusion using a more complex sketch with arcs and lines, extruded 5 mm. Chamfer two edges to round off the corners. Repeat the circular sketch and extrude it twice more at 11 mm each, and chamfer the final edges for a polished look.
