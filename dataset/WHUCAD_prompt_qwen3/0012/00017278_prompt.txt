Create a base extrusion by sketching a 95mm wide and 5mm tall rectangle on the XY plane and extruding it 96mm. Then, create three smaller rectangular extrusions along the length, each 8mm, 19mm, and 19mm wide, extruded 32mm deep. Finally, chamfer the edges and add a small protrusion at the end with a 7mm wide and 95mm tall sketch extruded 6mm.
