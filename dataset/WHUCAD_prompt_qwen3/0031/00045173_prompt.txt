Create a base shape by sketching eight lines on the XY plane forming a rectangle approximately 95mm wide and 57mm tall, then extrude it 96mm to form a solid body. Next, sketch four lines and two arcs on the same plane to create a smaller profile, about 76mm wide and 27mm tall, and extrude it 16mm to make a separate feature. Finally, chamfer the edges where the two features meet.
