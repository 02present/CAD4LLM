Create a base shape by extruding a square sketch of 95x95 mm to 96 mm height. Then add another extrusion of a smaller 95x63 mm sketch to the same height, followed by a third extrusion of a 48x95 mm sketch to 64 mm. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Finally, mirror and extrude a fourth 48x95 mm sketch to complete the structure.
