Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 32 mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Next, sketch two connected arc and line entities on the XY plane and extrude them 90 mm. Repeat this process with another set of arc and line entities, also extruding 90 mm. Finally, sketch multiple lines and an arc on the XY plane and extrude that sketch 56 mm to complete the model.
