Create a base shape by sketching six lines on the XY plane and extruding them 32 mm to form a new body. Then, sketch four lines on the same plane and extrude them another 32 mm to create a second body. Chamfer three edges of the first body and add a final chamfer to the top edge. Finally, sketch five lines on the XY plane again and extrude this sketch 96 mm to complete the design.
