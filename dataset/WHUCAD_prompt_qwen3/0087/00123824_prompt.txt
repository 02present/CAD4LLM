Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane and extruding them 5 mm to form a new body. Repeat the process with another set of four lines, extruding again 5 mm to create a second body. Chamfer each corner of both bodies four times, then add a circular sketch on the XY plane near the center and extrude it 5 mm to form a final feature, finishing with one more chamfer.
