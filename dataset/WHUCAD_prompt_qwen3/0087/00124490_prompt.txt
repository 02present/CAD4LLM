Create a base shape by extruding a sketch on the XY plane with a 95x63mm bounding box 3mm upwards. Chamfer the edges of this base. Then, create additional protrusions by extruding four more sketches on the same plane, with varying sizes and heights: two 3mm tall features with 95x95mm and 95x43mm bounding boxes, and two taller features 52mm high with 11x95mm bounding boxes.
