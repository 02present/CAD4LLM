Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm side length and extruding it 11mm. Repeat the same sketch and extrusion process to create a second identical body. Chamfer the edges of both bodies. Then, create two smaller rectangular sketches on the XY plane with a 95mm width and 12mm height, extrude them 11mm each, and chamfer their edges as well.
