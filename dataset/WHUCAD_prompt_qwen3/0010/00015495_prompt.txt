Create three extruded cylinders by sketching circles on the XY plane and extruding them outward. The first circle has a diameter of 80 mm and is extruded 64 mm, the second has a diameter of 86 mm and is extruded 80 mm, and the third has a diameter of 89 mm and is extruded 48 mm. All extrusions should create new bodies and be blind.
