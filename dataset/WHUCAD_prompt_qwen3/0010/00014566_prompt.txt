Create a base shape by sketching a 95mm by 66mm rectangle on the XY plane and extruding it 22mm. Then, create a second extrusion of 74mm using a smaller 95mm by 41mm sketch, followed by a third extrusion of 22mm with another 95mm by 66mm sketch, and finally a fourth extrusion of 3mm using a 95mm by 41mm sketch. Chamfer all edges where they meet.
