Create a base shape by sketching five lines on the XY plane and extruding them 1 mm to form a new body. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Repeat the process by sketching another set of five lines on the same plane and extruding them 1 mm to create a second body. Finally, sketch four lines on the XY plane again and extrude them 1 mm to form a third body.
