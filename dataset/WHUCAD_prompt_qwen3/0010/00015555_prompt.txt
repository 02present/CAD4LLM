Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 14 mm and extruding it 41 mm to form a cylinder. Then, add two chamfers to the edges. On the same plane, sketch another circle with a diameter of about 50 mm, extrude it 20 mm to create a second cylinder, and apply a chamfer to its edges as well. Finally, repeat the process for a third cylinder using the same dimensions as the second one.
