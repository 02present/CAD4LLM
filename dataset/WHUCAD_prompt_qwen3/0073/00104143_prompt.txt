Create a base shape by sketching a rectangle on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 21mm and extruding it 22mm. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch another rectangle on the XY plane measuring 18mm by 95mm and extrude it 47mm, followed by another chamfer. Repeat this process with rectangles of 95mm by 54mm and 95mm by 63mm, extruding them 22mm and 18mm respectively.
