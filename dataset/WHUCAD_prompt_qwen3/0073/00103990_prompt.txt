Create a base shape by sketching four lines and arcs on the XY plane, then extrude it 22 mm to form the first body. Add a second body by sketching more complex lines and arcs on the same plane and extruding it another 22 mm. Chamfer the edges where the two bodies meet. Finally, create a circular sketch on the XY plane and extrude it 10 mm to form the third body.
