Create a base by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 8mm and extruding it 22mm. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. On the same plane, sketch four lines forming a rectangular shape measuring approximately 95mm wide and 14mm tall, and extrude this 43mm. Repeat by sketching two more circles, each about 96mm wide and 96mm tall, and extrude both 26mm.
