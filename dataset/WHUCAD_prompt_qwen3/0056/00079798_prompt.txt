Create a base shape by sketching a square on the XY plane with 4 lines forming a 95x95 mm square and extruding it 52 mm. Then chamfer two edges of the resulting face. On the same plane, sketch a slightly smaller square with 4 lines forming a 92x95 mm rectangle and extrude it 26 mm. Repeat this process twice more with progressively smaller squares, each extruded 17 mm.
