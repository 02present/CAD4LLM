Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with a width of 95mm and height of 46mm, then extrude it 19mm to form the main body. Next, create a smaller sketch on the same plane with a height of 20mm and extrude it 3mm to form a protrusion. Chamfer the edges of the main body. Add additional protrusions by sketching and extruding smaller shapes with heights of 3mm, 3mm, 3mm, and 10mm respectively.
