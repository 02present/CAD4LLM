Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm side length and extruding it 32mm. Then, sketch a circle centered at the bottom of the square and extrude it 96mm upwards to form a column. Repeat the circular sketch on the same plane and extrude it another 32mm to create a second protrusion, finally chamfering the edges where the columns meet the base.
