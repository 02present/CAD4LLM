Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 8 mm and extruding it 8 mm to form a cylinder. Then, chamfer the edges. Next, sketch four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular shape approximately 95 mm wide and 11 mm tall, and extrude this sketch 22 mm to create a larger block. Finally, chamfer the outer edges of the block.
