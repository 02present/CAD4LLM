Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with 95mm sides and extruding it 96mm. Then, chamfer two corners of the resulting face. Repeat the process by sketching another square, extruding it 96mm, and chamfering again. Continue this pattern with two more sketches and extrusions, each time creating a new body and chamfering the last face of the latest extrusion.
