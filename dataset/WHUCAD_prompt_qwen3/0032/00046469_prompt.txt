Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 82 mm and extruding it 96 mm to form a cylinder. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Repeat the process by sketching another circle with a similar size and extruding it 121 mm to create a second cylinder, and finally sketch a slightly smaller circle and extrude it the same 121 mm to make a third cylinder.
