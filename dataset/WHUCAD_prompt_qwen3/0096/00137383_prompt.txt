Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 12 units and extruding it 61 mm. Then, add two chamfers to the edges. On the same plane, sketch a combination of arcs and lines forming a smaller profile, extrude it 96 mm, and repeat this process for another circular sketch and a final linear sketch, each extruded 96 mm, while adding a third chamfer to the last feature.
