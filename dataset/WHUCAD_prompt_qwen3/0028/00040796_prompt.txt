Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 63mm and extruding it 16mm. Chamfer the edges of the extruded face. Then, create a circular sketch centered at the bottom of the base, extrude it 48mm, and repeat the process with another circle to extrude 80mm. Finally, sketch three lines on the XY plane and revolve them around the central axis to form the final feature.
