Create three extruded cylinders by sketching circles on the XY plane and extruding them outward. The first circle has a diameter of 40 mm and is extruded 62 mm, the second has a diameter of 38 mm and is extruded 31 mm, and the third has a diameter of 96 mm and is extruded 21 mm. Each extrusion should create a new body and be blind-ended.
