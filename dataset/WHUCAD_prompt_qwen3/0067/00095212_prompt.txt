Create a base shape by extruding a circle with a diameter of about 72mm along the Z-axis for 11mm. Then add a smaller cylindrical feature by extruding a circle with a diameter of around 57mm for 8mm. Chamfer the edges of the first two features. Continue by creating a larger central cylinder with a diameter of 96mm and extrude it 35mm, then repeat this process three more times with the same diameter but increasing extrusion distances to 35mm, 35mm, and 81mm. Finally, chamfer the outer edges of the largest cylinder.
