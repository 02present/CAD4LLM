Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane with dimensions 95mm by 48mm and extruding it 24mm. Chamfer two corners of the extruded face. Then sketch another set of four lines on the same plane but larger, 95mm by 95mm, and extrude it 96mm. Finally, create two circular sketches on the XY plane with a diameter of 96mm and extrude each 24mm.
