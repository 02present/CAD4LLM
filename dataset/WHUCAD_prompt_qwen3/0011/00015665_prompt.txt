Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of approximately 15 mm and extruding it 20 mm to form a cylinder. Then, add two chamfers to the edges. On the same plane, sketch four lines forming a rectangular profile and extrude it 59 mm twice to create two parallel rectangular blocks. Finally, sketch three circles on the XY plane with a diameter of about 96 mm and extrude each 64 mm to form three identical cylindrical sections.
