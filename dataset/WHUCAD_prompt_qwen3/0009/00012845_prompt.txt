Create a symmetrical part by first sketching a circle on the XY plane and extruding it 82 mm to form a cylinder. Repeat this process for another circle on the same plane to create a second cylinder. Chamfer the edges where the two cylinders meet. Then, sketch four lines on the XY plane to form a rectangular shape and extrude it 20 mm to create a smaller rectangular block. Finally, sketch another set of four lines on the XY plane to form a vertical rectangle and extrude it 20 mm as well.
