Create a base shape by extruding a square sketch of 95x95 mm with a 2 mm depth. Then add smaller extrusions on the sides: a 5 mm deep feature along the top edge, a 5 mm deep feature along the bottom edge, a 10 mm deep feature on the left side, and a 1 mm deep feature on the right side. Finally, chamfer the corners of the main body and the smaller features.
