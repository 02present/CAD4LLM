Create a base body by extruding a circle with a 32mm diameter on the XY plane 20mm upwards. Repeat this process for four more circles positioned at different locations on the XY plane, each extruded 20mm upwards to form separate bodies. Chamfer the edges where these bodies intersect. Finally, create a sixth body by extruding a complex sketch composed of lines and arcs across the full width of the model, also 20mm upwards.
