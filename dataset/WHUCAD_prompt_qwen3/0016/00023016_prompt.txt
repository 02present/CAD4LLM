Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane and extruding them 23 mm to form a new body. Then, chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Repeat the process by sketching another set of four lines on the same plane and extruding them 23 mm to create a second body. Finally, sketch three lines on a larger XY plane and extrude them 23 mm to form the third and final body.
