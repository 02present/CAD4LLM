Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular outline and extruding it 3 mm to create a solid body. Then, chamfer the edges of this base. Next, sketch a circle centered at the bottom of the base and extrude it 122 mm upwards to form a cylindrical feature. Repeat the circular sketch on the same plane and extrude it another 122 mm to create a second identical cylinder.
