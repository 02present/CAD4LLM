Create a base shape by sketching a square with four lines and two arcs on the XY plane, then extrude it 25 mm. Add another sketch with five lines and an arc on the same plane, extruding it 25 mm as well. Chamfer the edges of the first extrusion. Continue by creating more complex sketches with arcs and lines, extruding each 25 or 45 mm to form the final structure.
