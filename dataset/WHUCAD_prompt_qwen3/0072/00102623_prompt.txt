Create a base shape by sketching four lines on the XY plane forming a square with a 95mm side length and extruding it 64mm. Then chamfer the edges. On the same plane, sketch another set of four lines forming a rectangle with a height of 72mm and extrude it 32mm, followed by another chamfer. Sketch a circle with a diameter of 96mm and extrude it 64mm, then repeat the circle sketch and extrude it 16mm.
