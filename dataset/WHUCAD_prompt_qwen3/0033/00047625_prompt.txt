Create a base shape by sketching three lines on the XY plane forming a rectangular outline and extruding it 48 mm to create a solid body. Then, chamfer the edges of this body. Next, sketch a circle on the XY plane and extrude it 96 mm to form another body, chamfering its edges as well. Finally, sketch another circle on the same plane and extrude it 32 mm to complete the model.
