Create a base shape by extruding a circle with a diameter of about 16 mm to a depth of 11 mm. Then, chamfer the edges of this feature. Repeat the process with a smaller circle, also extruded 11 mm deep, and chamfer its edges as well. Next, create two larger rectangular sketches and extrude them to depths of 53 mm and 51 mm respectively, forming the main body of the part.
