Create a base shape by sketching a square on the XY plane with 7 lines forming a 95x95 mm square and extruding it 21 mm. Then, chamfer two corners of the extruded face. Next, sketch a smaller rectangle on the XY plane measuring 48x95 mm and extrude it 83 mm. Repeat this process again with a mirrored sketch positioned below the original to form a symmetrical structure.
