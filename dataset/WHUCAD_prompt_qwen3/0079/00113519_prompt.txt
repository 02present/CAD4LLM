Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a diameter of about 92 mm and extruding it 31 mm to form a cylinder. Then, add a chamfer to the edge. Repeat by sketching another circle of the same size on the XY plane and extrude it 125 mm to create a larger cylinder. Finally, sketch a smaller circle with a diameter of about 32 mm and extrude it 125 mm to make a shorter, narrower cylinder.
