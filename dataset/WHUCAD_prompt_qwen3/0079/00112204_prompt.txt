Create a base shape by sketching a circle on the XY plane with a bounding box from 0 to 96 in X and -48 to 48 in Y, then extrude it 5 millimeters to form the first body. Chamfer the edges of this extrusion. Repeat the process by sketching another circle on the same plane, extruding it 16 millimeters to create a second body, and do the same again for a third extrusion of 16 millimeters.
