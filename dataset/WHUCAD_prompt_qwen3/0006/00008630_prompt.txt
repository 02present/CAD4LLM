Create a base shape by extruding a sketch on the XY plane with four lines, 64mm wide and 17mm tall, 2mm deep. Repeat this process for three more sketches, each slightly larger in width and height, and extrude them 2mm deep as separate bodies. Chamfer the edges of the first two extruded bodies. Finally, create two more vertical extrusions based on narrower sketches, also 2mm deep, and chamfer their edges as well.
