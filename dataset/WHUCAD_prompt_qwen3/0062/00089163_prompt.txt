Create a base shape by sketching an arc and line pattern on the XY plane, then extrude it 3mm to form a small feature. Add a chamfer to one edge. On the same plane, sketch another arc and line pattern slightly smaller and lower, and extrude it 127mm to create a larger body. Repeat the sketch and extrusion process one more time with a slightly adjusted position and extrude again 127mm to complete the main structure.
